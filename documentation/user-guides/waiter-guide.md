# Waiter guide

For waiters using the **Waiter POS** (`/waiter`), designed for tablets. Sign in with your own account; waiters land on
the POS automatically. Technical details: [`../project/dashboards/WAITER_MODULES.md`](../project/dashboards/WAITER_MODULES.md).

## Start of shift

The **Dashboard** shows your current shift, your active orders, ready orders, free and occupied tables, and bill
requests. Quick actions: New Order, View Tables, My Orders, Request Bill.

## Taking an order

1. **Tables:** tap a free table → **Start new order** (or use **New Order** and pick the table there).
2. Choose products by category or search; set quantities with − / +, add notes or special instructions per item, the
   guest count and, if allowed, the customer.
3. Check the total and press **Send order**. The kitchen and the cashier are notified at once.
   - If the table was taken in the meantime, or a price changed, the POS tells you and asks you to review.
   - Tapping twice can't create two orders.

## Adding items

From **Tables** or **My Orders**, choose **Add items** on your own unpaid order (not after the bill is requested). Items
already sent can't be edited or removed by a waiter; ask the cashier or manager.

## Following the order

- **Kitchen Status** shows New → Accepted → Preparing → Ready → Served. You can't change kitchen statuses; when your
  order is **Ready**, collect it and press **Mark served**.
- **Notifications** tell you when your order is accepted, ready or cancelled, when the bill is acknowledged and when
  payment is complete.

## The bill

1. **Bills → Request bill.** Cashiers and managers are notified.
2. You can cancel the request only before the cashier starts and before any payment (if the manager allows it).
3. When the bill is fully paid, the receipt is available to print.

Waiters never record, change or delete payments.

## Good to know

- Colleagues' tables are view-only unless the manager allows adding items to them.
- The header shows **Live / Reconnecting**; the POS catches up by itself after a lost connection.
- **My Profile:** change your name, phone and password. **Logout** asks for confirmation and clears an unsent draft.
